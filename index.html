Canham Recreational Facility

Canham Recreational Facility is located behind the Intramural Building on the University of Michigan campus. The facility is used for competitive and club sports and is no longer open to the general public.

Competitive and Club Sports

Canham is used by competitive and club sports at the University of Michigan. Sports that have used the facility include diving, water polo, and triathlon clubs.

Location

Canham Recreational Facility is located behind the Intramural Building on the University of Michigan campus.

Not Open to the Public

Unlike many recreational facilities on campus, Canham is no longer open to the general public. Access is primarily associated with the groups and activities that use the facility.

Fun Fact

Michael Phelps swam in Canham Recreational Facility.
